**Vision:** To become the company most admired for its creativity and understanding of customers.

**Mission:** VPS opens easy access to investment, finance, and consumer products/services, delivering outstanding, sustainable value to customers, partners, and the community — following the philosophy "opening access to financial investment for everyone."

**Tone of voice:** Professional but approachable · Friendly & natural · Confident but not arrogant · Inspiring

## 2. Logo

**Concept:** The icon combines a square (Technology) and a circle (People) into concentric "ripple" rings, symbolizing continuous growth, unity, and security. The wordmark "vps" is set in lowercase with rounded corners for approachability, paired with a modern sans-serif typeface. The violet icon color references the "price ceiling" (giá trần) concept from stock trading, reflecting the brand's core promise of delivering the best results.

**Construction / grid:**
- Base module unit = `X`.
- Icon proportions: ring elements at `0.5X`, `X`, `2.5X`.
- Logo structure (icon + wordmark): wordmark block ≈ `12X` wide, icon ≈ `4X`, with `0.3X` gaps between elements; overall height reference ≈ `4X`.
- Logo + tagline lockup: tagline sits below at reduced scale, overall width ≈ `17X`; tagline must stay clearly smaller than the logo but legible, proportion balanced (not overpowering).

**Clear space:** Minimum clear space around the logo equals one icon-width (`X`) on all sides.

**Minimum size:** 25mm (print) / 75px (digital).

**Color versions:**
| Version | Usage |
|---|---|
| Black "vps" + violet icon | On white/light backgrounds |
| White "vps" + violet icon | On black/dark backgrounds |
| All-white (icon + wordmark) | On violet background |
| Reverse/one-color (black or `#F7F7F7` white) | Restricted cases only — e.g., on busy photo backgrounds or colored backgrounds |

Note: the icon should generally always appear in violet; the wordmark toggles between black/white/`#F7F7F7` depending on background. Never alter the icon's color value.

**Logo on photography:** Prefer standard full-color logo on clean/high-contrast areas of an image; use the all-white wordmark version on darker photo backgrounds. Avoid placing the logo on low-contrast, busy, or overly detailed image areas, or too close to/overlapping a subject.

**Logo misuse — never:**
1. Recolor the logo
2. Add an outline/stroke around it
3. Add text around it
4. Stretch or distort it
5. Change the relative size between icon and wordmark
6. Apply drop shadows
7. Rotate it
8. Substitute a different typeface
9. Apply gradient effects
10. Redraw it in any way — always use the supplied master files

**Special production techniques** (for physical branded items): foil stamping, embossing/debossing, engraving, laser etching, leather stamping, etc.

## 4. Typography

### 4.1 Primary typeface — Forma DJR Display

Modern, geometric sans-serif chosen for its clean, squared letterforms; works for both Vietnamese and English. **Requires a commercial license to use.**

Weights available: **Light** & Light Italic · **Regular** & Italic · **Medium** & Medium Italic · **Bold** & Bold Italic

**Recommended tracking:** increase letter-spacing to **+30** (in design-tool tracking units) so characters don't feel too tight.

**Usage hierarchy:**
| Weight | Use case |
|---|---|
| Bold / Bold Italic | Words needing emphasis |
| Bold / Medium | Taglines and headlines |
| Regular | Subtext / long-form body copy |
| Light / Light Italic | Captions |

### 4.2 Fallback typeface — Arial

Used only where Forma DJR Display isn't available: Microsoft Word/PowerPoint documents, email. **Do not** use Arial for high-visibility/high-reach media (billboards, print ads, posters, social media, website) — Forma DJR Display is required there.

Weights: Bold, Regular.

### 4.3 Type usage rules (do's / don'ts)

- Don't combine typefaces with excessive contrast
- Don't stretch or condense type
- Don't use heavy weights for long passages of body text
- Don't color long passages of body text

## 5. Brand Motif / Graphic Signature (Motip)

Four expandable-ring motif options built from the logo's "ripple" concept, used as decorative graphic devices in layouts:

| Option | Vietnamese name | English |
|---|---|---|
| A | Vượt trội | Superior |
| B | Bứt phá | Breakthrough |
| C | Lan toả | Spread / Ripple |
| D | Tăng tốc | Accelerate |

**Graphic treatments (can mix/match per format):** flat shapes/blocks (Mảng) · blurred/gradient strokes (Nét loang) · line art (Nét) · 3D renders (used when 2D graphics get too detailed/colorful; typical for event backdrops and document covers for a cleaner, more modern feel).

**Rule:** primary violet must occupy at least 20% of a campaign design's total color area, even in special/seasonal campaign treatments where fonts, effects, imagery, and color may otherwise flex.

## 6. Photography / Imagery Style

- **Framing:** full-body or 2/3-body shots of people
- **Cast:** mixed gender, Asian, across three age bands — 25–30 (young/energetic), 30–50 (mature/professional), 50–70 (experienced)
- **Emotion:** positive — happy, excited, open to new experiences; approachable and engaging
- **Wardrobe:** brand-color-aligned or neutral/business-formal (suits, office attire)
- **Pose/action:** clear, purposeful poses that communicate a specific message

**Avoid:** mismatched/inappropriate clothing, overly staged shots, underexposed/dark images, blurred or distracting visual effects.

## 8. Layout & Applications

### 8.1 Social media template structure
- Image occupies **30–60%** of the layout
- Image should be clean, uncluttered, minimal effects, focused on the main subject
- Headline placement is flexible but must stay legible — avoid decorative effects on headline text
- Standard elements: main image, headline, subtext, CTA button, hotline/website line, logo (with safe zone around it — 65px margin noted in one template spec)

## 9. Usage Governance

- Any use of the VPS brand must strictly follow the official guideline and requires approval from the **VPS Marketing Brand Management Department**.
- Only the officially specified color palette may be used — no substitutions or new colors.
- Only the specified fonts may be used per platform/media type.
- Tone and communication style must stay consistent with core brand values.
- All imagery must reflect the brand spirit, be high quality, and suit the target customer.
- Detailed rules apply per channel (website, social, out-of-home, print) to avoid inconsistent or misleading brand representation.

**Contact for details:** VPS Marketing Brand Management Department — marketing@vps.com.vn
